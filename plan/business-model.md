# Business Model

## Company concept

A single-category consumables MLM (working example: plant-based nutrition
and wellness supplements) — consumables drive repeat retail purchase
independent of recruitment, which is the healthiest MLM revenue pattern
regulators look for.

## Revenue streams

1. **Retail sales** — product sold at retail price to non-distributor
   customers, no signup required. This is the primary revenue target.
2. **Distributor wholesale purchases** — distributors buy at a wholesale
   discount for personal use and resale. Capped per distributor per month
   to discourage "inventory loading."
3. **Starter kit** — one-time low-cost kit (target: under $100) containing
   product samples + business tools. Deliberately priced low and framed as
   optional to avoid pay-to-play characteristics.

## Target market

- Primary customers: health-conscious consumers, 28–55, discovered via
  distributor social sharing and referral.
- Primary distributors: side-hustle-motivated individuals already using
  and liking the product, recruited from the retail customer base first
  (not cold-recruited from outside).

## Positioning

"Buy it because you'd buy it anyway; sell it because you already do."
The opportunity is pitched as a *side income from something you use*, not
a wealth-building or investment vehicle — this framing carries through to
`marketing-strategy.md` and the required disclosures in
`compliance-legal.md`.

## Product policy

- Buyback policy: company repurchases unsold, resalable inventory from
  departing distributors at 90%+ of original price, for at least 12 months
  after purchase (matches typical state anti-pyramid statutes' buyback
  requirements).
- No mandatory minimum purchase to remain "active" beyond a low threshold
  tied to genuine retail activity, not stockpiling.
