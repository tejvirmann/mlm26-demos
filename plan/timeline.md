# Delivery Timeline (example, 6 months to public opportunity launch)

## Phase 0 — Foundations (Weeks 1–3)
- Finalize business model, comp plan draft, legal engagement kickoff
- Tech stack decisions locked (`tech-stack.md`)
- Brand identity + product photography

## Phase 1 — Product-first site (Weeks 4–8)
- Public marketing site: home, shop, product detail, checkout
- Payments integration, tax integration
- Legal review of all public copy
- **Milestone: soft launch, DTC sales only, no distributor program**

## Phase 2 — Distributor core (Weeks 9–16)
- Distributor auth, portal dashboard, replicated storefront engine
- Genealogy/downline data model + views
- Commission engine v1 (batch, auditable) + staging test with synthetic data
- Comp plan finalized and legally reviewed
- Income Disclosure Statement drafted (pre-launch, conservative modeling)
- **Milestone: founding distributor cohort onboarded (invite-only)**

## Phase 3 — Compliance hardening (Weeks 17–20)
- Marketing-asset library + approval workflow
- Compliance admin dashboard (retail-vs-recruitment ratio, flagged content)
- Payout integration (Tipalti/Hyperwallet) + 1099 pipeline
- Full commission engine audit against founding cohort's first real period

## Phase 4 — Public opportunity launch (Weeks 21–24)
- Public `/opportunity` pages live, IDS published with real first-period data
- Distributor locator, public storefronts indexed
- Support processes, help center live
- **Milestone: public launch**

## Post-launch (ongoing)
- Quarterly rank re-verification cycle
- Quarterly compliance audit (see `compliance-legal.md`)
- Annual IDS refresh
