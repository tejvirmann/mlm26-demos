# Compensation Plan

> Designed to satisfy `compliance-legal.md` first; commission math is
> secondary to the structural constraints below.

## Structural rules (non-negotiable)

1. **70% retail rule**: a distributor cannot earn recruitment/rank bonuses
   in a period unless they can show retail sales (or personal/family
   consumption, capped) of at least 70% of the inventory they purchased
   that period. Modeled on the DSA/FTC-referenced guideline used to
   distinguish legitimate MLMs from pyramid schemes.
2. **No pay-to-recruit**: zero commission is paid simply for signing
   someone up. Commission triggers only on a *sale* (retail or qualifying
   wholesale) somewhere in the downline.
3. **Compression, not infinite depth**: bonuses pay out to a maximum of 7
   levels deep, with "compression" skipping inactive distributors, capping
   the incentive to build width over unsustainable depth.

## Plan structure

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Retail profit | Distributor keeps the margin between wholesale and retail price on direct sales |
| Team bonus | 5–10% on qualifying sales volume from levels 1–3 of their downline |
| Leadership bonus | Unlocked at "Director" rank+, paid from a capped company-wide leadership pool, not from downline recruitment volume |
| Rank advancement | Based on rolling 90-day retail volume, not one-time recruitment spikes |

## Ranks (example ladder)

1. Distributor (entry)
2. Senior Distributor — 90-day retail volume threshold
3. Team Lead — + 2 active personally-sponsored distributors
4. Director — + team volume threshold, unlocks leadership pool
5. Executive Director
6. National Director

Each rank requires **maintenance criteria re-verified quarterly** — ranks
are not permanent titles, preventing "rank inflation" that misleads new
recruits about typical outcomes.

## What we will NOT do

- No guaranteed or implied income figures in any comp plan literature.
- No "fast start" bonus that pays more for recruiting than for the
  equivalent dollar amount of retail sales.
- No requiring a minimum personal purchase that exceeds what a distributor
  can reasonably resell or use, to earn ordinary commission.

## Worked example (illustrative only — not a claim of typical results)

A distributor who personally sells $500/mo retail and has 3 active
personally-sponsored distributors each doing the same nets approximately:
retail profit (~$150 at 30% margin) + team bonus (~5% of $1,500 downline
volume = $75) = **~$225/month**, before any leadership bonus. This example
must ship alongside the full Income Disclosure Statement, never alone.
