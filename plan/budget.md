# Budget Estimate (illustrative order-of-magnitude, USD)

> Ranges only — actual figures depend on team location, build-vs-buy
> choices, and whether teams are in-house or contracted.

| Category | Phase 0–1 | Phase 2–3 | Phase 4 + ongoing/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering (2–4 devs) | $40k–$80k | $80k–$160k | $20k–$40k/mo |
| Design/brand | $10k–$25k | $5k–$10k | — |
| Legal (MLM/comp-plan counsel) | $15k–$30k | $10k–$20k | $2k–$5k/mo retainer |
| Payments/payout processor fees | — | setup ~$1k–$5k | ~2–3% of processed volume |
| Hosting/infra | $0.5k–$2k/mo | $1k–$3k/mo | $2k–$5k/mo at scale |
| Compliance tooling/admin | — | $5k–$15k | $1k–$3k/mo |
| Marketing (product-first launch) | $10k–$30k | $20k–$50k | ongoing per CAC targets |

## Key cost drivers to watch

- Commission engine correctness — under-invest here and the cost shows up
  later as legal exposure and distributor trust loss, not just rework.
- Legal spend front-loaded is cheaper than legal spend after a regulator
  inquiry — do not defer the `compliance-legal.md` review to "later."
- Payout processor choice affects both fees and how much 1099/KYC work is
  built vs. bought.
