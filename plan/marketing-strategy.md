# Marketing Strategy

## Positioning recap

Lead with product, not opportunity. See `business-model.md`.

## Launch sequence

1. **Product-first soft launch** — sell direct-to-consumer with no
   distributor program live yet, to prove product-market fit and generate
   genuine customer testimonials (not recruited-distributor testimonials).
2. **Founding distributor cohort** — invite the most engaged existing
   customers (not cold recruits) to become the first distributors.
   Small, high-touch cohort so support and compliance processes can be
   validated before scaling.
3. **Public opportunity launch** — once the Income Disclosure Statement
   has at least one real reporting period of data behind it.

## Channels

- Organic social via distributor storefronts (compliant, disclosed)
- Content marketing / SEO on product education (blog), not income claims
- Paid acquisition aimed at **retail customers**, not recruits — recruits
  should come from happy customers, not cold opportunity ads
- Referral program for retail customers (separate from distributor comp
  plan, simple store credit)

## Content rules for distributor-generated marketing

- Every post mentioning the business must carry a disclosure
  ("#ad" / "Independent Distributor" per FTC Endorsement Guides)
- No screenshots of commission checks/deposits presented as typical
- Approved claims library only (see `/portal/marketing-assets` in
  `site-map.md`) — distributors pick from pre-approved copy/images rather
  than freehand claims
- Spot-check process feeding into `compliance-legal.md` audit cadence

## Metrics to track

- Retail customer acquisition cost vs. distributor acquisition cost
- % of revenue from repeat retail customers
- Content compliance flag rate per distributor cohort
- Organic share rate of approved marketing assets
