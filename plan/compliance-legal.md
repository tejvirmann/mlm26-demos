# Compliance & Legal

> This document is a planning aid, not legal advice. Before launch, engage
> counsel experienced in direct-selling / MLM law (state anti-pyramid
> statutes vary) and have the Direct Selling Association's Code of Ethics
> reviewed against the final comp plan and marketing copy.

## Why this document exists

The line between a lawful MLM and an illegal pyramid scheme is almost
entirely about **whether money flows from real sales to end consumers, or
from recruiting people into a purchasing chain**. Every other document in
this folder is written to keep the product on the legal side of that line.

## Key regulatory references to check against before launch

- FTC Business Opportunity Rule (16 CFR Part 437)
- FTC's Business Guidance on Multi-Level Marketing (the "MLM Guide")
- Relevant state anti-pyramid-promotional-scheme statutes (varies by state
  of incorporation and of distributor residence)
- FTC Endorsement Guides (for distributor social media claims)
- Koscot test / Webster test case law factors, as a design checklist

## Required disclosures on the website

1. **Income Disclosure Statement (IDS)** — published, dated, updated
   annually, linked from every page that mentions the opportunity,
   showing the actual distribution of earnings across all distributors
   (including the % earning $0), not just top earners.
2. **Terms of the buyback/refund policy** — visible pre-signup, not buried
   in a PDF after payment.
3. **Distributor social-media disclosure requirement** — training +
   enforced policy that distributors disclose their affiliate/distributor
   status per FTC Endorsement Guides on every post mentioning the product
   or opportunity.
4. **No health/medical claims** without substantiation appropriate to the
   product category — for a supplements company this also touches
   FDA/DSHEA rules, separate from FTC/MLM rules.

## Process gates before go-live

- [ ] Legal review of final compensation plan math and structure
- [ ] Legal review of all public-site copy, especially opportunity pages
- [ ] Income Disclosure Statement drafted from real (or, pre-launch,
      conservatively modeled) data and reviewed by counsel
- [ ] Distributor agreement / policies & procedures drafted and reviewed
- [ ] State-by-state registration check (some states require MLM-specific
      business licenses or bonding)
- [ ] Data privacy review (distributor PII, downline data, payment data —
      PCI-DSS if processing cards directly)

## Ongoing compliance operations (post-launch)

- Quarterly audit of retail-vs-recruitment revenue ratio
- Spot audits of distributor social posts for unapproved income claims
- Annual refresh of the Income Disclosure Statement
- Complaint intake process feeding back into policy enforcement
